About Senior Citizen Helpline

Dedicated to the safety, dignity and well-being of senior citizens across India.

Our Mission

To provide 24/7 emergency support for elderly citizens in distress.
To ensure access to medical, emotional and social assistance.
To promote awareness about senior citizen rights and welfare schemes.
